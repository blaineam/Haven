1.1.8 — adding a friend sticks, and calls keep your mic:
• Adding a friend now keeps trying until they answer instead of getting one attempt. Requests sent over mobile data reach people who never got them.
• If you and someone had removed each other, neither could reconnect. Now either of you can ask again.
• Switching apps during a call no longer silently mutes you.
• Turning your camera on mid-call sends real video, not a black square.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Haven really free?

Yes, on every platform: no ads, no tracking, no subscription and no in-app purchases. Haven runs no servers of its own, so there's nothing to bill you for. Development is funded by people who choose to support it.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no email and no phone number. Your identity is a set of keys on your own device, and you bring your people in with an invite link.

What is a relay, and do I need one?

A relay holds your circle's sealed messages while friends are offline, and it can't read them. Any Haven app can be one: turn on “Be your circle's relay” in Settings on a device you leave running, or run a standalone relay.

Can a relay see my IP address?

Briefly, while it moves your bytes, as any internet service would. It never logs or stores it, and there's no name to tie it to. To hide your IP entirely, run Haven behind your own VPN or host your own relay.

Has Haven been audited?

Not by a paid outside firm; Haven is free and unfunded. We ran our own adversarial audit, with no critical or high findings. Anyone is welcome to review the code and report findings privately to apps@wemiller.com.

Is Haven available in the European Union?

Yes, in every EU country except France, where apps that use encryption need a separate government declaration. The source code is open to everyone on GitHub.

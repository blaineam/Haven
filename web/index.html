Privacy, in plain words

The honest version, no fine print.

Here's how Haven keeps your life yours — what stays private, and the honest limits too.

What stays private

  • Every post, message, photo, video and call is encrypted on your device before it's sent.
  • The secret keys that unlock your content stay on your own devices — in the Secure Enclave on Apple devices, the OS secure store elsewhere. The only way they sync between your devices is Apple's end-to-end-encrypted iCloud Keychain, which we can't read either. We never hold them.
  • There's no central account, no profile database, no advertising ID, no analytics.
  • Choose your own storage — a Haven relay you (or a friend) run, or your own S3 bucket (S3/R2/B2). We run no storage and hold no credentials.

The honest limits

  • A relay (an optional helper) can see that some encrypted bytes passed through, but never what they say.
  • To deliver bytes to you, a relay briefly handles your IP address — that's just how the internet works. It's never logged, and never tied to who you are.
  • Friends never see each other's IP when a relay is in the path — only the relay does, briefly. For full IP hiding today, run Haven behind your own VPN; a built-in onion/Tor mode is on the roadmap, not shipped.
  • If someone steals your unlocked, malware-infected device, they have your device. No app on earth fixes that.
The promise: never logged, never linked to you, optionally fully hidden. For the full picture, read the threat model and architecture docs.
Want it even more reliable?

Be the bridge for your circle.

Every Haven app can be a relay: flip “Be your circle's relay” in Settings and your device quietly holds your circle's sealed messages for friends who are offline — running reliably in the background, always end-to-end encrypted with post-quantum cryptography it can never open. Leave a Mac or desktop app running and your whole circle gets more resilient. No terminal, no server, nothing to install.

Turn yours on

1. Open Haven on a device you leave running
2. Settings ▸ Relays → “Be your circle's relay”
3. Done — friends' apps find it automatically

Prefer a terminal? The standalone relay 
curl -fsSL https://wemiller.com/apps/haven/relay/install.sh | sh
haven-relay run --link "haven-relay://circle#…"

One command to install, one to go live — for a Pi or server with no Haven app. No ports, no domain, no cloud required.

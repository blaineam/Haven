1.1.3 — relays that behave:
• Big photos and videos pick up where they left off instead of restarting from scratch.
• Receiving a large video no longer loads the whole thing into memory, so size limits are gone.
• Sending keeps pace with your connection rather than queueing the entire file at once.
• Fixed media that never arrived because the same transfer kept starting over.
• Setting a relay as your default now serves every circle, not just one.

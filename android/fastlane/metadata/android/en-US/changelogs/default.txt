2.1.0 — Calls you can see, and an optional wake-up.
• Outgoing calls now show Calling… / Connecting… / Connected, play a dial tone, ring the iPhone, and hang up after 45 seconds if unanswered.
• Optional push wake-up in Settings (off by default) for phones that close Haven in the background.
• Stay connected no longer runs out after a few hours, so messages and calls keep arriving.
• Incoming calls show the caller's name, and Android Auto notifications are tidier.
